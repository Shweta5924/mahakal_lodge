Welcome to MAHAKAL LODGE (महाकाल ईन लॉज २), your serene sanctuary in Nanded, Maharashtra.

Conveniently situated near Balaji Mandir in Old Mondha, MAHAKAL LODGE offers modern accommodations tailored for pilgrims visiting Takht Sachkhand Sri Hazur Abchalnagar Sahib, business travelers, and vacationing families. Our rooms are equipped with contemporary cove lighting, spotless marble floors, high-speed Wi-Fi, and 24-hour dedicated front-desk service to ensure your trip is both restful and unforgettable.
